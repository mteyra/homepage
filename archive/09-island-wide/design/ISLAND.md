# Island

The selected reference is Rauno’s black-and-white 2023 homepage: one spacious opening and a floating navigation capsule. Marc specifically rejected the clutter within the reference’s deeper sections. This iteration borrows the restraint and navigation principle, with its own sculptural identity and content structure.

## Composition

An off-white field, graphite and silver monogram, large name and short present-tense introduction. A small CV link stays at the top. Four explicit destinations sit in a single satin graphite island: About, Studies, Experience and Projects. A plus button grows that same shell upwards into links and motion preferences.

The three solid parts of the M begin slightly separated and rotated. Each aligns with one click; the completed form settles onto its shadow. No course, link or biographical information is locked behind completion. The metaphor belongs to Marc’s identity instead of inventing a building or fictional environment.

## Reading

The sculpture leaves the stage when a reading view opens. The reading canvas uses the viewport width with consistent outer gutters. Three aligned columns hold course or experience groups; projects use two columns. Heading context and the current employer share these column alignments. Paragraphs retain a comfortable reading measure within them. Tablets reflow to two columns and phones to one. There are no nested tabs.

- Studies gives exact course names equal weight, grouped by computation, biology and medicine, and independent study.
- Experience begins with the current employer, then groups practical accounts by teams, science and care, and robotics and games. A visible contribution line gives the short account; an inline disclosure reveals context and outcome.
- Projects stays deliberately compact, with four source-backed entries and direct links.

The public content source is unchanged. No portrait, origin, invented article, unverified job title or claimed live travel connection has been added.

## Motion and control

A fine light underline slides between labelled destinations on a dark satin surface. The navigation uses text labels and an integrated plus control. The outer island grows while the links remain anchored in place. Reading views enter with a short vertical movement; their text is never left transparent during an interrupted animation. The sculpture responds to pointer movement and selection, then resolves its pieces with a small physical settle.

Keyboard, reduced motion, manual pause, browser history, WebGL failure and hidden-page completion have dedicated handling. Normal body scrolling is used in reading views. Mobile navigation retains every label and the document’s bottom padding clears the floating island.
