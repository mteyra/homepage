# Island

The selected reference is Rauno’s black-and-white 2023 homepage: a spacious opening and floating navigation. The aim is a restrained personal introduction with 3D that supports clear exploration.

## Composition

An off-white field, graphite and silver monogram, large name at the left and a short introduction at the right. A CV link remains at the top. Four explicit destinations occupy a thin translucent navigation island, with a small active dot. A plus button grows the same shell upwards into profile links and motion preferences.

The three parts of the M begin separated. Each aligns with one click. Completion never gates access to content.

## Inner views

Each view uses the width of the viewport. A left column holds the actual subject, present-tense context and one restrained sculpture. A right column holds its information. The content takes precedence on phones, with a compact sculpture beside the introduction.

- Studies: three intersecting rings select computation, biology and medicine, or independent study. Exact course names and certificate links remain visible in the chosen group.
- Experience: three layered pieces select teams and prototypes, science and care, or robotics and games. The current employer is always visible. Each entry gives a contribution line and expands its source-backed account.
- Projects: four frames accompany four concise entries. Pointer and keyboard focus connect the object to each project; links lead directly to source, presentation or the CV.

The text index provides all sculpture selections with standard keyboard controls. No information is locked behind a 3D gesture. There is no portrait, origin, invented article, unverified job title or claimed live travel feed.

## Motion

Chapter objects animate gently when selected or approached, then settle. They do not spin continuously. Hidden views stop rendering and destroyed views release their graphics resources. Reduced motion, manual pause and WebGL fallback retain the same content and controls. The navigation stays anchored while its menu grows.
