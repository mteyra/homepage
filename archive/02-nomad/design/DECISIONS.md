# NOMAD — design study 02

## User direction

The previous editorial portfolio was rejected as ordinary. This rebuild treats spatial exploration as the information architecture. No portrait, no articles or empty writing section, and no claimed live map connection. The subsequent content pass uses the supplied CV versions and LinkedIn export.

## Experience

A single connected architectural station contains a workshop, research observatory, geographic outpost and orientation hall. Camera journeys and mechanical reveals give each destination a different spatial character. A permanent plain-language dock, quick index, plan view, keyboard controls and deep links provide immediate access without requiring exploration skills.

Palette: midnight cobalt, porcelain and vermilion. Material detail, lighting and movement carry the personality; there are no stacked homepage sections, scroll effects or a mandatory boot sequence. On mobile, the selected building remains above an independently scrolling reading sheet.
