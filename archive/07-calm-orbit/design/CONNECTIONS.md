# Connections

## The change in direction

The current request takes Orbit’s spatial navigation towards an easy puzzle and explicitly removes personal geography. The new homepage revolves around three interests with evidence behind each: computation, biology and medicine, and practical work. The content source preserves the named coursework rather than compressing it into a generic “AI student” label.

## A physical action with a physical result

Three concentric rings share a fixed alignment mark. Each starts one or two magnetic steps away. A connected ring locks and carries a pulse inward; the final connection unfolds the same assembly into layers using the same camera and materials. Reset closes it again. The experience has no timer, score or precision penalty.

The mechanism is deliberately bounded. It provides curiosity and a spatial introduction, while a professor or employer can immediately read the degree, current employer, coursework or CV. No content depends on winning the puzzle. The full record uses restrained horizontal rules, explicit dates and institutional attribution, reflecting the strongest observed organizational qualities of the source CVs.

## Content hierarchy

1. Marc, current degree and current employer.
2. Exact academic courses and selected real practical experiences through the three rings.
3. The full studies record, ten experiences with contributions visible while skimming, four brief public/completed projects and certificate source links.

Current status is separated from the dated CV. University courses are labeled coursework without inferring grades or completion; PH526x is audited. CS50’s professional certificate and its two courses are not represented as separate extra study. Shadowing and team contribution remain explicit. The disputed year of Icosagen shadowing is omitted.

No personal origin, travel, spoken-language biography, homepage portrait, authored article claim or invented employment title is used. Projects support the person’s story rather than becoming the entire interface.

## Interaction and access

- Pointer drag, keyboard and ordinary buttons use one authoritative puzzle state.
- The main action guides a visitor to the next unsolved ring after a connection.
- The same ring tabs move above the canvas on phones so selection and turning remain together.
- Pause and reduced motion settle into actual final object states.
- A failed WebGL context uses an SVG mechanism with the same state and controls.
- The canvas sleeps when idle and suspends rendering offscreen or in a hidden tab.
- Direct-reading anchors transfer keyboard focus to the requested section.
