# Calm Orbit

The previous Connections design exposed the puzzle, several content controls and too much supporting material together. The user preferred the earlier Orbit's clarity. This revision returns to its spatial composition while keeping the fuller academic and practical record.

## Four subjects, one reading surface

About introduces Marc and his current degree and employer. Studies shows named courses in three groups. Experience groups ten accounts by Science & care, Teams, and Robotics & games. Projects contains four brief descriptions and their source links.

The chapter links are ordinary visible navigation. The orbit provides a second, physical way to choose the same subject. Its moving leaves correspond directly to the four destinations. There is no fictional environment or invented narrative around the record.

Only one chapter and one group are visible. On desktop the chapter heading and group tabs stay in place during selection. Full experience accounts use a single dismissible dialog. URL hashes preserve deep links and browser history.

## Keep play separate

The optional alignment puzzle occupies its own view. It has one instruction, one instrument and one set of controls. The profile and CV are available independently. No profile panel, course list or project showcase competes with the puzzle.

## Visual decisions

Warm metal, muted blue glass, dark space and one consistent typeface. Quiet navigation, fine separators and enough room for real text. On phones the object is smaller within chapters so the reading material takes priority. Text remains visible during motion; reduced-motion preferences and manual pause stop decorative movement.

No portrait, personal-origin story or invented publications are added. Institutions are named as source attribution. More content can be added to the existing subject groups without introducing new simultaneous panels.
