# One person, three ways in

## The changed center of gravity

The preceding iteration made the projects the experience. This pass starts from Marc: present direction, intellectual interests, background, communication and life across places. A growing project list can remain concise without defining the whole homepage.

## What the supplied material actually shows

The CV variants were already reconciled for content; this pass also revisited their visual construction. Repeated horizontal rules, deliberate date rails, clear institutional attribution, restrained accent colors and visible credential links suggest that hierarchy and evidence matter. Those are observed patterns, not a claim to know the author's private intentions.

The clinical application compresses the record around biology, lab/clinical exposure and practical data work, omitting the portrait and much of the robotics/game history. The fuller CV restores broader activities. This supports selective presentation rather than treating every omission as something to fill back in.

The early narrative connects AI, robotics, physics, space and an interest in how systems work. Later evidence adds biomedical study, interdisciplinary teams, pitching and current employment. The LinkedIn introduction also uses the author's own playful “Hello :D”. The formal record and approachable tone coexist.

No raw CV or LinkedIn export is copied into the new editions. No likes on LinkedIn are represented as authored articles, and no research or employment affiliation is inferred from a short learning experience.

## Visitors and the information they need

| Visitor | Useful first impression | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Professor | Current degree, academic interests, learning trajectory and accurate exposure | Read background, course evidence or CV |
| Employer | Name, location, present study/work, practical contribution and communication | CV, LinkedIn, concise selected work with source links |
| Classmate or friend | An approachable person with interests, languages and places in their story | Explore curiosity or human background |
| Parent or nontechnical visitor | A comprehensible direction and sense of development | Read the path here without decoding tools or jargon |
| GitHub visitor | The wider person behind the repositories | A small selection, then the growing source collection |

The sites do not make visitors identify their role. Each offers natural routes to the same person.

## Three independent approaches

**01 / Profile.** A composed static personal document. Strong serif identity, midnight ink, warm paper-colored type, copper rules and a disciplined source/date structure. It is deliberately complete without motion or JavaScript.

**02 / Orbit.** A physically rendered personal instrument. The five selectable leaves organize Now, Study, Experience, Roots and Making. Turning the object changes the chapter; readable controls, motion pause and a reading mode support direct access.

**03 / Hello :D.** A nonlinear introduction in six scenes. The author's own greeting becomes a draggable question selector. Whole compositions and color change with the subject; visitors can scrub the timeline, follow interests or switch among language greetings. The ordinary question index and arrow keys make every destination reachable without performing a gesture. No chat model or generated conversation is implied.

Projects receive one section in each. Full project experiences remain external links, and the previous immersive project-window approach is preserved in version04.

## Scope

This is a local design comparison. The existing public homepage is unchanged. No exact job title, unverified date, invented article, live travel connection or additional project architecture has been added. The February CV snapshot is labeled where it is contextualized; current employer information comes from the later LinkedIn evidence.
