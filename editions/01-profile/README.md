# Profile · Edition 01

A static, person-first personal profile for Marc Teyra Galiano. No JavaScript, build step, external font service or embedded application is required.

The composition uses a fixed reading order: current study and work, selected learning experiences, further study, concise public projects, international background, and contact. The dates and small labels echo the clarity of Marc's CV; the broad typography and quiet copper rules give the profile its own identity.

Content is based on the public February 2026 CV and the verified shared profile in `../../assets/content.js`. The Siemens Healthineers entry deliberately gives the organisation and start date without inventing a job title. Work shadowing, online certificates and independent programming are identified explicitly. The linked Aloha report is team material. Icosagen is intentionally undated because the CV and LinkedIn disagree about its year.

The CV, Manrope, JetBrains Mono and favicon are shared local assets. Newsreader is bundled under the SIL Open Font License in `fonts/`.

Open through a local static server at `/editions/01-profile/`. Internal page anchors work without scripts. The footer links return to the shared edition collection or open the other two designs.

Verified in Chrome at 320, 390, 700, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels, with no horizontal overflow. All text is at least 10 pixels; body copy is 13–17 pixels. Native skip-link focus, section navigation and browser Back were checked. All local links, fonts and the CV return successfully.

`preview.webp` is the desktop review image; `preview-mobile.webp` shows the mobile hero; `preview-full.webp` shows the full composition.
