health-sync

A small personal tool I wrote for myself. It signs in to my own Google account and copies my own Fitbit data from the Google Health API to my own laptop, twice a day, so I can keep and study it there.

The source code is public, in mteyra/nixos-config (modules/health-sync.py and modules/health.nix). It uses Google’s open-source ghealth tool.

Privacy policy

  • Who uses it: only me. health-sync isn’t offered to anyone else, and only my own Google account signs in to it.
  • What it reads: the health and fitness data in my Google account that I allow when I sign in, such as steps, heart rate and sleep. It only reads; it never changes or deletes anything in the account.
  • Where the data goes: into files on my own laptop. health-sync sends it nowhere else. It isn’t shared, sold, or used for advertising, and there are no analytics or trackers.
  • Sign-in: the Google sign-in token stays on my laptop as well.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time in my Google Account’s third-party connections. Deleting the files on the laptop deletes the copy.
  • Contact: github.com/mteyra.

If health-sync ever starts sending data anywhere else, this page will say so first. Last updated 18 November 2026.
